Camping Setup & Gear

Simple Car Camping Setup

You don't need an expensive RV or a specialized camper van to start your journey into the great outdoors. Most road trips can be done comfortably by transforming your current vehicle—whether it’s a hatchback, SUV, or sedan—into a functional sleeping space with just a few basic, low-cost adjustments. We will show you how to optimize your limited space to set up a cozy and secure interior, allowing you to bypass pricey hotels and wake up right at the doorstep of a breathtaking National Park. By mastering a few simple DIY techniques, you can turn your everyday car into a mobile basecamp that fits a student budget perfectly.

Essential Budget Gear

Affordable gear examples.

  • LED Lanterns: Use rechargeable ones that can also charge your phone.
  • Portable Stoves: Single-burner butane/propane stoves are cheap and easy to use.
  • Sleeping Pads: Foam or inflatable pads offer great insulation at a low cost.
  • Thrifted Kits: Use stainless steel pots from thrift stores instead of pricey gear.

Camping Budget Tips

Tips for saving money.

  • Free Campsites: Use apps to find "dispersed camping" on public lands.
  • National Parks Pass: Buy the annual pass if visiting three or more parks.
  • Cook Your Own Meals: Prep simple food at your car to avoid tourist prices.
  • Travel with Friends: Split the costs of gas, entry fees, and ice as a group.

Making Your Gear Last for Years

Investing in budget-friendly gear is only the first step; practicing proper maintenance is what ensures your equipment remains reliable for years to come. To prevent mold and fabric deterioration, always make sure to dry your tent and sleeping bag completely before storing them away. Developing a consistent post-trip habit—like cleaning your stove and inspecting your vehicle—can prevent unexpected failures during your next big adventure. By treating your simple gear with respect, you avoid costly replacements and keep your limited student budget focused on reaching your next destination. Taking these extra steps helps build a sustainable kit that grows with your experience and keeps you on the road longer.
