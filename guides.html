Yellowstone National Park

Admission Cost: $35 for vehicle. 7-day pass. 3,500 square miles of wilderness atop a volcanic hotspot. Yellowstone features canyons, rivers, forests, hot springs and geysers including its most famous, Old Faithful. Hundreds of animal species, including bears, wolves, bison, elk and antelope.
