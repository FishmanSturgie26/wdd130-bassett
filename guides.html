National Parks and natural Wonders

Yellowstone National Park

Admission Cost: $35 for vehicle. 7-day pass. 3,500 square miles of wilderness atop a volcanic hotspot. Yellowstone features canyons, rivers, forests, hot springs and geysers including its most famous, Old Faithful. Hundreds of animal species, including bears, wolves, bison, elk and antelope.

In the corner of Wyoming, Montana, and Idaho

Grand Canyon National Park

Admission Cost: $35 for vehicle, $30 for motocycle. 7-day pass. A massive canyon carved by the Colorado river, showing layers of red bands of rock. This monument showcases millions of years of geologic history. The canyon averages 10 miles across, a mile deep, and is 277 miles in length.

Northern Arizona

Yosemite National Park

Admission Cost: $35 per vehicle for US resident. 7-day pass. With over a thousand square foot area, this National Park is famed for it's giant sequioa trees, the most massive trees on the planet. Featuring 'Tunnel View' a magnificent view between cliffs overlooking the forest.

California's Sierra Nevada Mountains
